## Why

The control model protects human ownership, but operators must edit Markdown/YAML directly and `auto` immediately resumes scheduling. Status does not expose the full queue or decision history.

## What Changes

- Add preflight/doctor and safe auto preview.
- Add queue inspection and issue lifecycle commands.
- Add structured status output and attention-friendly diagnostics.

## Non-goals

- No web UI, IDE integration, or autonomous human-decision replacement.
